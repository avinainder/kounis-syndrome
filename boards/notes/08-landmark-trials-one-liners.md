# 08 – Landmark Trials: One-Liners to Memorize

Format: **Trial** – population – comparison – result – exam takeaway.

## Stable CAD / revascularization strategy
- **COURAGE** (2007) – stable CAD – PCI+OMT vs OMT – no ↓death/MI – OMT first.
- **BARI 2D** – diabetes stable CAD – revasc vs OMT – no mortality diff; CABG stratum ↓MI.
- **FREEDOM** – diabetes MVD – CABG vs PCI – CABG ↓death/MI (18.7% vs 26.6% at 5 y), ↑stroke.
- **SYNTAX** – 3VD/LM – CABG vs PES – CABG better overall (5-y MACCE 26.9% vs 37.3%); PCI OK
  if SYNTAX ≤22.
- **EXCEL** – LM, SYNTAX ≤32 – PCI(EES) vs CABG – 5-y death/stroke/MI 22% vs 19.2% (NS);
  all-cause death higher with PCI (secondary); controversy about MI definitions.
- **NOBLE** – LM – PCI vs CABG – 5-y MACCE 28% vs 19%: CABG better (non-procedural MI, revasc).
- **PRECOMBAT** – LM – PCI(SES) vs CABG – non-inferior at 1 y, 10-y similar death.
- **ISCHEMIA** – moderate-severe ischemia, no LM – invasive vs conservative – no ↓CV death/MI at
  3.2 y median; better angina QoL; ↓spontaneous MI later, ↑procedural MI early.
- **ISCHEMIA-CKD** – eGFR <30/dialysis – invasive no benefit; more stroke/dialysis initiation.
- **ORBITA** – single-vessel, sham – PCI ≈ sham for exercise time (6 weeks).
- **ORBITA-2** – sham, no antianginals – PCI ↓angina symptom score.
- **REVIVED-BCIS2** – EF ≤35% viable myocardium – PCI+OMT vs OMT – no ↓death/HF hosp.
- **STICH/STICHES** – EF ≤35% – CABG vs OMT – 10-y mortality ↓ with CABG.
- **FAME** – MVD – FFR-guided vs angio-guided PCI – ↓MACE, fewer stents.
- **FAME 2** – FFR ≤0.80 – PCI+OMT vs OMT – ↓urgent revasc; no death/MI benefit.
- **FAME 3** – 3VD – FFR-PCI vs CABG – PCI failed non-inferiority at 1 y (10.6% vs 6.9%).
- **DEFER** – FFR ≥0.75 – deferral safe 15 y.
- **DEFINE-FLAIR / iFR-SWEDEHEART** – iFR non-inferior to FFR at 1 y; less adenosine discomfort.
- **FUTURE** – all-comers FFR strategy – stopped early, no benefit, mortality signal.
- **TARGET-FFR** – post-PCI FFR-guided optimization – more patients reach ≥0.90; no MACE diff.

## ACS
- **SHOCK** – AMI cardiogenic shock – early revasc vs medical – 6-mo/1-y mortality ↓.
- **CULPRIT-SHOCK** – AMI shock MVD – culprit-only vs immediate multivessel – culprit-only
  ↓death/RRT (45.9% vs 55.4%).
- **IABP-SHOCK II** – AMI shock – IABP no mortality benefit.
- **DanGer Shock** (2024) – STEMI shock – Impella CP vs standard – ↓180-d death (45.8% vs
  58.5%), ↑bleeding/limb ischemia/RRT.
- **ECLS-SHOCK** – AMI shock – VA-ECMO vs standard – no mortality benefit, more bleeding.
- **COMPLETE** – STEMI MVD – complete (staged) vs culprit-only – ↓CV death/MI (7.8% vs 10.5%).
- **PRAMI / CvLPRIT / DANAMI-3-PRIMULTI** – complete revasc ↓MACE (mostly revasc).
- **MULTISTARS AMI / BIOVASC** – immediate = staged complete revasc.
- **FLOWER-MI** – STEMI non-culprit – FFR-guided vs angio-guided – no benefit of FFR.
- **FIRE** – MI ≥75 y – physiology-guided complete revasc ↓death/MI/stroke/revasc.
- **TASTE / TOTAL** – STEMI – routine aspiration no benefit; TOTAL ↑stroke.
- **DANAMI-3-DEFER** – deferred stenting no benefit.
- **OAT** – occluded IRA 3–28 d, stable – PCI no benefit.
- **TRANSFER-AMI / STREAM** – pharmacoinvasive (angio 6–24 h after lysis) ≈ primary PCI;
  STREAM ↑ICH in >75 y → half-dose TNK.
- **TIMACS / VERDICT** – NSTE-ACS – early (<24 h) invasive benefits high-risk (GRACE >140).
- **COACT / TOMAHAWK** – cardiac arrest without STEMI – no benefit of immediate angiography.
- **HORIZONS-AMI** – STEMI – bivalirudin vs UFH+GPI – ↓bleeding, ↓death; ↑acute ST.
- **MATRIX** – ACS – radial ↓mortality/bleeding; bivalirudin ≈ UFH; post-PCI bivalirudin
  infusion ↓ST.
- **BRIGHT-4** – STEMI – bivalirudin + high-dose infusion vs UFH – ↓death/bleeding.
- **VALIDATE-SWEDEHEART** – bivalirudin ≈ UFH (radial, potent P2Y12).
- **RIVAL** – radial vs femoral – overall NS; benefit in STEMI and high-volume centers.
- **SAFARI-STEMI** – radial vs femoral in STEMI – no difference (stopped early).
- **EROSION** – OCT plaque erosion – antithrombotics without stent feasible.

## Antiplatelet/anticoagulant
- **CURE / PCI-CURE** – clopidogrel + ASA in NSTE-ACS ↓MACE.
- **CURRENT-OASIS 7** – 600 mg clopidogrel load better in PCI; aspirin dose no difference.
- **TRITON-TIMI 38** – prasugrel vs clopidogrel ACS-PCI – ↓MI/ST, ↑bleeding; harm with prior
  stroke/TIA, ≥75 y, <60 kg.
- **PLATO** – ticagrelor vs clopidogrel ACS – ↓CV death/MI/stroke, ↓death; ↑non-CABG bleeding,
  dyspnea; aspirin >100 mg interaction (North American paradox).
- **ISAR-REACT 5** – prasugrel > ticagrelor in ACS-PCI (6.9% vs 9.3%).
- **ACCOAST** – prasugrel pretreatment in NSTEMI – no benefit, ↑bleeding.
- **TRILOGY ACS** – prasugrel in medically managed ACS – no benefit.
- **CHAMPION PHOENIX** – cangrelor vs clopidogrel – ↓periprocedural MI/ST.
- **DAPT** – 30 vs 12 months – ↓ST/MI, ↑bleeding; DAPT score.
- **PEGASUS-TIMI 54** – ticagrelor 60/90 mg 1–3 y post-MI – ↓MACE, ↑bleeding.
- **TWILIGHT** – ticagrelor mono after 3 months DAPT – ↓bleeding, no ↑ischemia.
- **TICO / STOPDAPT-2 / SMART-CHOICE / GLOBAL LEADERS** – P2Y12 monotherapy after 1–3 mo.
- **MASTER DAPT** – HBR, 1-month DAPT non-inferior.
- **HOST-EXAM** – after 12 mo DAPT: clopidogrel mono > aspirin mono.
- **ADAPTABLE** – 81 = 325 mg aspirin.
- **WOEST / PIONEER AF-PCI / RE-DUAL PCI / AUGUSTUS / ENTRUST-AF PCI** – AF + PCI: dual
  therapy (OAC + clopidogrel) ↓bleeding vs triple; AUGUSTUS 2×2 apixaban better, aspirin harmful.
- **AFIRE** – AF + stable CAD >1 y – rivaroxaban mono ≥ combination.
- **COGENT** – clopidogrel + omeprazole – no ↑CV events, ↓GI bleeding.
- **TAILOR-PCI / POPular Genetics** – genotype-guided: TAILOR negative primary; POPular safe
  de-escalation in STEMI.
- **COMPASS / VOYAGER PAD** – low-dose rivaroxaban + ASA ↓MACE/MALE, ↑bleeding.
- **COLCOT / LoDoCo2** – colchicine ↓MACE post-MI/CCS.
- **ARMYDA / NAPLES II** – statin loading ↓periprocedural MI.

## Devices, imaging, lesion subsets
- **DKCRUSH-V** – distal LM true bifurcation – DK crush ↓TLF vs provisional (5.0% vs 10.7%).
- **EBC MAIN** – LM bifurcation – provisional non-inferior (numerically better) vs 2-stent.
- **NORDIC / BBC ONE / CACTUS** – non-LM bifurcation – provisional ≥ 2-stent.
- **DEFINITION II** – complex bifurcation (DEFINITION criteria) – 2-stent ↓TLF.
- **ULTIMATE** – IVUS-guided DES ↓TVF (1 and 3 y).
- **RENOVATE-COMPLEX-PCI** – IVUS/OCT-guided complex PCI ↓TVF.
- **ILUMIEN IV** – OCT: larger MSA, ↓ST; no TVF difference.
- **OCTOBER** – OCT-guided bifurcation ↓MACE.
- **OCTIVUS** – OCT non-inferior to IVUS.
- **ADAPT-DES** – IVUS use ↓ST/MI (registry).
- **PROSPECT / PROSPECT II** – vulnerable plaque (PB ≥70%, MLA ≤4, TCFA; maxLCBI4mm >324) predicts events.
- **PREVENT** (2024) – preventive PCI of vulnerable plaques ↓composite (driven by revasc).
- **ROTAXUS / PREPARE-CALC** – rota vs balloon before DES: better acute gain, no late benefit.
- **ECLIPSE** (2024) – orbital atherectomy vs balloon prep – no benefit.
- **DISRUPT CAD III** – coronary IVL 92% procedural success, low MACE.
- **DECISION-CTO / EuroCTO** – CTO PCI: no hard-endpoint benefit; EuroCTO ↑QoL.
- **EXPLORE** – CTO PCI after STEMI – no LVEF benefit.
- **SAFER** – SVG PCI – distal occlusion EPD ↓MACE 42%.
- **DIVA** – SVG PCI – DES = BMS.
- **ISAR-CABG** – SVG – DES ↓TLR at 1 y.
- **AGENT IDE** – coronary ISR – paclitaxel DCB > balloon.
- **RIBS IV/V** – DES-ISR: EES ≥ DCB; BMS-ISR: equal.
- **BASKET-SMALL 2** – small vessels – DCB non-inferior to DES.
- **LEADERS FREE** – HBR – polymer-free DCS + 1 mo DAPT > BMS.
- **ABSORB III/IV** – bioresorbable scaffold – ↑target lesion failure/ST vs EES; withdrawn.
- **XIENCE 28/90, Onyx ONE** – short DAPT after modern DES in HBR.
- **SYMBIOT / BARRICADE** – covered stents in SVG: no benefit/harm.
- **TASTE, TOTAL** – above.
- **FAUST** – US-guided femoral access ↓complications.
- **DISCO RADIAL** – distal radial ↓RAO; equal success.

## Structural
- **PARTNER 1B / 1A / 2A / 3** – inoperable / high / intermediate / low risk TAVR.
- **CoreValve US Pivotal / SURTAVI / Evolut Low Risk** – self-expanding across risk.
- **NOTION** – all-comers TAVR vs SAVR 10-y similar.
- **SMART** – small annulus – Evolut > SAPIEN for valve hemodynamics/dysfunction.
- **PROTECTED TAVR** – Sentinel – no ↓overall stroke; ↓disabling stroke (secondary).
- **BHF PROTECT-TAVI** – cerebral protection no benefit.
- **POPular TAVI** – aspirin alone (A) / OAC alone (B) ↓bleeding, no ischemic penalty.
- **GALILEO** – rivaroxaban after TAVR – ↑death/bleeding; ↓leaflet thrombosis (imaging substudy).
- **ATLANTIS** – apixaban after TAVR – not superior.
- **EVEREST II** – MitraClip vs surgery – surgery better MR reduction; TEER safer.
- **COAPT** – secondary MR (disproportionate) – TEER ↓HF hosp/death.
- **MITRA-FR** – secondary MR – neutral.
- **RESHAPE-HF2** – TEER in functional MR – positive.
- **CLASP IID** – PASCAL non-inferior to MitraClip (primary MR).
- **TRILUMINATE** – tricuspid TEER ↓TR, ↑QoL; no mortality/HF hosp benefit.
- **TRISCEND II** – EVOQUE TTVR – ↓TR, ↑QoL.
- **PROTECT AF / PREVAIL / PRAGUE-17 / Amulet IDE / PINNACLE FLX / OPTION** – LAAO.
- **RESPECT / CLOSE / REDUCE / DEFENSE-PFO** – PFO closure ↓recurrent stroke; ↑AF.
- **PREMIUM / MIST** – PFO closure for migraine: negative.
- **SEATTLE II / ULTIMA / OPTALYSE / FLARE / EXTRACT-PE / PEERLESS / PEITHO / MOPETT** – PE.
- **ATTRACT** – DVT CDT – no ↓PTS overall.
- **CREST / ACT I / SPACE / EVA-3S / ICSS / CREST-2 (pending)** – carotid.
- **ASTRAL / CORAL** – renal stenting no benefit.
- **BEST-CLI / BASIL-2** – CLTI: bypass with GSV better; infrapopliteal endovascular ≥ bypass.
- **VOYAGER PAD** – rivaroxaban after revasc ↓MALE.
- **PARTNER 3 / Evolut LR 5-y** – outcomes converge; durability ongoing.
