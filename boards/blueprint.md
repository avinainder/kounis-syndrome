# ABIM Interventional Cardiology Certification Blueprint (effective January 2026)

Converted from the official PDF (interventional-cardiology-cert.pdf). Use this as a checklist:
tick a topic only when you can answer a question on it cold. ABIM notes that actual exam content
may vary, that questions may require integration of significant clinical trial results, and that
health-equity content clinically important to the discipline is included.

**Format.** Up to 220 single-best-answer questions, about 35 unscored. Most items are patient
scenarios asking what the physician does: make a diagnosis, order and interpret tests, recommend
treatment, assess risk and prognosis, apply epidemiologic principles, or explain pathophysiology.
Pictorial material includes coronary angiograms, ventriculograms, IVUS, nuclear perfusion, CT, MRI,
ECG, echo, and peripheral vascular imaging. Tutorial: https://www.abim.org/about/abim-exams/exam-tutorials/

## Case Selection and Management (20%)

### Chronic ischemic heart disease (6%)
- [ ] Clinical characteristics (demographics and comorbidities)
- [ ] Laboratory abnormalities and cardiac catheterization (hematology, coagulation, chemistry)
- [ ] Renal insufficiency and cardiac catheterization
- [ ] Noninvasive testing before diagnostic catheterization
- [ ] Selection of treatment modality: interventional, surgical, medical
- [ ] Preoperative cardiac evaluation for noncardiac surgery
- [ ] Preoperative revascularization before noncardiac surgery

### Unstable angina and NSTEMI (4%)
- [ ] Evaluation and risk stratification
- [ ] Pharmacologic management
- [ ] Timing of cardiac catheterization
- [ ] PCI in UA/NSTEMI

### STEMI (6%)
- [ ] STEMI systems of care
- [ ] Primary PCI: procedure, stents, thrombectomy, outcomes
- [ ] Right ventricular infarction
- [ ] Multivessel PCI
- [ ] Primary PCI following cardiopulmonary arrest
- [ ] STEMI differential diagnosis; acute aortic dissection
- [ ] Therapeutic hypothermia
- [ ] Fibrinolytic therapy; transfer for PCI; rescue PCI
- [ ] Surgical therapy in STEMI
- [ ] Medical management after STEMI

### STEMI complications (4%)
- [ ] Shock
- [ ] Cardiac arrest
- [ ] Electrophysiologic complications; emergency pacing
- [ ] Acute respiratory distress
- [ ] Mechanical complications (MR, VSD, rupture, pseudoaneurysm)
