## My rules (one line each, in my own words)
1. Prasugrel is never given before the anatomy is known in NSTE-ACS; the load happens in the lab when PCI is chosen (ACCOAST harm, ISAR-REACT 5 benefit).
2. Atrial-level shunt: mixed venous is (3×SVC + IVC)/4, never the RA; PvO2 is assumed 98% unless there is a right-to-left shunt; Qp/Qs = (SaO2 − MV)/(PvO2 − PaO2).
4. Donor vessel to a CTO: collateral flow lowers donor FFR (falsely positive); it rises after the CTO is opened. Culprit vessel in STEMI: stunned microcirculation raises FFR (falsely negative).
5. Occluded infarct artery, stable and pain-free, more than 24 hours out: medical therapy (OAT). Reopen only for ongoing ischemia, instability, heart failure or arrhythmia.
3. Air kerma 5 Gy = document, counsel, skin check at 2–4 weeks; 15 Gy = sentinel event report; fluoro time alone never decides.
